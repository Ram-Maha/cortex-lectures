§ 02 — About the Series

A gathering for those who study the most complicated three pounds of matter in the known universe.

The Cortex Lectures bring leading scientists in cognition, neuroscience, vision, multisensory perception, motor learning, language, and the philosophy of mind into conversation with our department roughly twice a month throughout the academic year. Every talk is open to students, faculty, and visiting researchers from across the university.

Our speakers are chosen by a rotating committee of graduate students and faculty. We deliberately balance computational and experimental, human and animal, theoretical and applied. The result is a series where you might encounter a single-cell electrophysiologist and a Bayesian modeler at the same coffee table afterward — which is exactly the point.

Recordings are archived (with speaker permission) and made available to the public. We welcome speaker suggestions from anyone in our community — students, postdocs, faculty, or visiting scholars.
